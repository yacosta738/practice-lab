Use this hub to combine the official blueprint, independent explanations, video instruction, and
Practice Lab's own study materials. External resources remain on their publishers' sites; we link to
them rather than republishing their lessons, videos, or question banks. Evidence labels describe
what was checked about each resource, not a guarantee of continued availability; see
the [evidence status convention](../source-register/#evidence-status-convention).

## Suggested study sequence

1. Read the [exam blueprint and coverage map](../exam-blueprint-and-coverage/) and
   the [25-competency index](../ccdv-f-indice-de-certificacion/).
2. Study the highest-weight domains first, while keeping a review pass for all eight domains. Start
   with
   the [D2 Applications and Integration notes](../d2-applications-and-integration-moc/), [D5 Model Selection and Optimization notes](../d5-model-selection-and-optimization-moc/),
   and [D1 Agents and Workflows notes](../d1-agents-and-workflows-moc/).
3. Use the applied workshops for the remaining priority
   gaps: [D6](../d6-prompt-and-context-engineering-moc/), [D8](../d8-tools-and-mcps-moc/), [D7](../d7-security-and-safety-moc/), [D3](../d3-claude-code-moc/),
   then [D4](../d4-eval-testing-and-debugging-moc/).
   The [Knowledge Gap Backlog](../knowledge-gap-backlog/) records the evidence and scope of each.
4. Use the external lessons or video to clarify a topic, then verify version-sensitive details in
   the [official technical references](../source-register/).
5. Take a Practice Lab quiz, explain why the correct option fits and why each distractor does not,
   then close gaps with the [practical labs](../start-here/).

The official passing score is scaled; a practice percentage is not an official pass prediction. See
the [exam guide](../exam-blueprint-and-coverage/) for the scoring caveat and
the [exam decision guide](../exam-decision-guide/) for a repeatable question-review method.
