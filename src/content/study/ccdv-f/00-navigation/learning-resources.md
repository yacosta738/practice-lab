Use this hub to combine the official blueprint, independent explanations, video instruction, and Practice Lab's own study materials. External resources remain on their publishers' sites; we link to them rather than republishing their lessons, videos, or question banks.

## Official sources

- [Anthropic Partner Academy — Claude Certified Developer Foundations](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/path/claude-certified-developer-foundations) — the official course. Lesson access may require an account or enrollment.
- [CCDV-F Exam Guide, version 1.0](../ccdv-f-exam-guide.pdf) — the authority for exam scope, objective weights, format, and scoring. Check Anthropic's current exam page for later revisions.
- [Claude Developer learning path](https://anthropic-partners.skilljar.com/path/claude-developer) — a broader official learning path; it is not a substitute for the CCDV-F exam blueprint.

## Independent lessons and diagnostic

- [AI Cert Guides — free CCDV-F lessons](https://aicertguides.com/ccdv-f/learn) — the publisher describes 25 lessons mapped to the exam guide's sub-skills.
- [AI Cert Guides — free diagnostic](https://aicertguides.com/ccdv-f/diagnostic) — use it to identify topics for review, not as a prediction of the official result.
- [AI Cert Guides terms](https://aicertguides.com/terms) — review the publisher's current terms before using or adapting its material. Their lessons and questions are not copied into Practice Lab; paid mock exams remain on the publisher's site.

These are independent preparation resources, not official Anthropic material. Their coverage and product details can change, so use the official blueprint and documentation as the authority for exam scope and technical behavior.

## Suggested study sequence

1. Read the [exam blueprint and coverage map](../exam-blueprint-and-coverage/) and the [25-competency index](../topics/ccdv-f-indice-de-certificacion/).
2. Study the highest-weight domains first, while keeping a review pass for all eight domains. Start with the [D2 Applications and Integration notes](../d2-applications-and-integration-moc/), [D5 Model Selection and Optimization notes](../d5-model-selection-and-optimization-moc/), and [D1 Agents and Workflows notes](../d1-agents-and-workflows-moc/).
3. Use the external lessons or video to clarify a topic, then verify version-sensitive details in the [official technical references](../source-register/).
4. Take a Practice Lab quiz, explain why the correct option fits and why each distractor does not, then close gaps with the [practical labs](../start-here/).

The official passing score is scaled; a practice percentage is not an official pass prediction. See the [exam guide](../exam-blueprint-and-coverage/) for the scoring caveat and the [exam decision guide](../exam-decision-guide/) for a repeatable question-review method.
