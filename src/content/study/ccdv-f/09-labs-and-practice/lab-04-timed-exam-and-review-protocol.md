**Goal:** turn the unofficial practice bank into a repeatable diagnostic, not a misleading pass
guarantee.

## Exam facts from official guide

- 53 questions, multiple-choice and multiple-response.
- 120 minutes, proctored.
- 720/1000 passing **scaled score**; do not equate to 72% raw.
- Domain weights approximate and actual item allocation varies.

## Practice routine

1. Sit one timed 53-question session without external notes using
   the [interactive HTML quiz](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ZEnvO9nZSFz61k3exGBMf1MMEISgzbHs/view).
2. Record errors by domain and underlying concept (e.g., tool_result protocol, caching invalidation,
   injection boundary), not just question IDs.
3. Review the related notes; reproduce one failing behavior in code for each important error.
4. Retake with distinct or shuffled questions; watch for memorization and require explaining why
   distractors fail.
5. Aim for strong and consistent per-domain practice performance; an 80% target is a private study
   heuristic, not an Anthropic pass rule.
